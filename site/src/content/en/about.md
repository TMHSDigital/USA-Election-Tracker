USA Election Tracker is a free, public reference for the November 3, 2026 elections for U.S. House, U.S. Senate, and governor. It is built to be usable and trusted by people of all political views.

## What you will find

- A page for every race, with candidates as listed by state election officials, federal campaign finance totals, a district map, and past results for the seat.
- A page for every state, with its races and links to its official election office.
- A ballot lookup that finds your congressional district from your location, without sending your location anywhere.
- Links to official voting information and official results for every state.
- Every dataset we use, available to download.

## What you will not find

No forecasts, polling averages, race ratings, commentary, comments, or accounts. We do not call races.

## How it is built

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. Data is collected by scheduled jobs that check every source before publishing it. The code and data are public in the project repository.

## Privacy

Page views are counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies. Locations used in the ballot lookup stay in your browser.
