USA Election Tracker is an independent project. It is not affiliated with any government agency, political party, candidate, or campaign, and it does not endorse anyone.

Data on this site is compiled from public sources and may contain errors, omissions, or delays. Official candidate lists and official results come only from state and local election officials. Always confirm important information, such as where and how to vote or who is on your ballot, with your state or local election office.

Election night figures on this site, where shown, are unofficial. They are copied from official sources on a schedule and lag those sources. Results are not final until certified by election officials.

Every page shows when its data was last updated, for each source. If a source could not be updated, the page says so.

If you find an error, please report it using the link at the bottom of every page. Corrections are logged publicly with dates.

Page views are counted with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies. Addresses and locations you enter or share in the ballot lookup are processed only in your browser and are never sent to us.
